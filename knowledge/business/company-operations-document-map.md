# Company operations document map

## The point in one line
A project-based Indian business (contracting, engineering, fabrication, installation, interiors) runs on a predictable chain of documents from lead to closure; when Brimpa builds an ERP, CRM or project tool for such a client, this list is the module and form checklist to walk through in discovery.

## The lists, exactly as shown (order kept)
1. **Company registration**: PAN, TAN, GST registration, Udyam registration, MSME, trade licence, shop and establishment, bank account, digital signature (DSC), IEC (import export), PF registration, ESIC registration. (Verified and sorted by need in business/india-company-incorporation-checklist.md.)
2. **Company setup**: organisation structure, departments, employee hiring, HR policy, office setup, computer system, ERP, file management, email system, document numbering system.
3. **Company administration**: office documents, file system, approval process, workflow, authorisation matrix, official communication, meeting minutes, circular, office order.
4. **Marketing and sales**: lead, enquiry, RFI, RFI reply, RFQ, budgetary quote, estimate, cost estimate, budget, quotation, revised quotation, commercial offer, technical offer, proposal, proforma invoice.
5. **Tender process**: tender, NIT, BOQ, technical bid, price bid, EMD, bid security, PBG, security deposit, LOI, LOA, agreement.
6. **Project planning**: scope of work, BOM, BOQ, estimate, rate analysis, DPR, method statement, risk assessment, project schedule, project plan.
7. **Procurement**: vendor registration, vendor comparison, comparative statement, purchase requisition, purchase indent, purchase order, amendment PO, work order, service order, material receipt.
8. **Stores**: GRN, material inward, material outward, stock register, bin card, inventory register, store issue slip, gate pass, return note.
9. **Site execution**: daily progress report, daily work report, site log book, attendance, material consumption, site instruction, site memo.
10. **Quality control**: ITP, inspection report, checklist, NCR, CAPA, test report, FAT, SAT.
11. **Accounts**: tax invoice, commercial invoice, GST invoice, debit note, credit note, receipt voucher, payment voucher, journal voucher, cash voucher, contra entry, e-way bill.
12. **Project completion**: commissioning report, completion certificate, handover report, warranty certificate, O&M manual, final bill, closure report.
13. **Sales service**: AMC, preventive maintenance, breakdown maintenance, service report, complaint register, spare parts management, customer feedback.
14. **Company growth**: branch office, ISO certification, audits, financial planning, expansion, branding, digital marketing, CRM, ERP upgrade, new product development.
15. Closing caption: learn to run a company before opening one; the job was for learning the process, not the money.

## Abbreviations
RFI request for information; RFQ request for quotation; NIT notice inviting tender; BOQ bill of quantities; BOM bill of materials; EMD earnest money deposit; PBG performance bank guarantee; LOI letter of intent; LOA letter of acceptance; DPR detailed project report (in some firms daily progress report); GRN goods receipt note; ITP inspection and test plan; NCR non-conformance report; CAPA corrective and preventive action; FAT/SAT factory/site acceptance test; O&M operation and maintenance; AMC annual maintenance contract.

## How to use it at Brimpa
- Discovery for an ERP or project-management build: go department by department and ask "which of these do you issue today, on paper, Excel or Tally, and who approves it?" Each document is a form, a numbering series, an approval step and a PDF template.
- Build order that usually pays first: enquiry -> quotation (with revisions) -> PO/work order -> GRN/stock -> DPR -> invoice. Tender and QC modules come later.
- Document numbering and the authorisation matrix (who can approve what amount) are cross-cutting; design them first.
- Accounts entries (vouchers, e-way bill, GST invoices) usually stay in Tally or Zoho Books; integrate rather than rebuild.
- For hospitals the same skeleton appears in purchase, stores and biomedical-equipment AMC; see domains/hospital-management-modules.md.

## Caveats
- The lists reflect an engineering or contracting business; a clinic or retail shop needs only a subset.
- "Proforma invoice", "tax invoice" and "GST invoice" overlap: under GST a tax invoice is the GST invoice; proforma is a quote, not a tax document.

## What the source got wrong
- "MSME" and "Udyam registration" are listed separately; they are the same registration.
- "Commercial invoice" in Indian domestic trade is usually an export document; domestic sales use the tax invoice.
- Otherwise it is a checklist, not claims; no numbers to verify.
