# Incorporating a private limited company in India: the real minimum

## The point in one line
SPICe+ gives you incorporation, PAN, TAN, EPFO and ESIC numbers in one filing; after that the hard deadlines are the first auditor (30 days) and the commencement-of-business declaration INC-20A (180 days, not 120); most other "registrations" an agent sells are optional, free on the government portal, or only needed once you hit a threshold.

## What SPICe+ (INC-32) gives in one go
- Certificate of incorporation with CIN; DIN for new directors (directors need a Class 3 DSC to sign).
- **PAN and TAN** issued with the certificate (mandatory Part B services).
- **EPFO and ESIC registration numbers** (mandatory Part B services, even with zero employees; contributions start only when the Acts apply: EPF at 20+ employees, ESIC at 10+).
- Through AGILE-PRO-S: GST registration (optional), professional tax where the state levies it, and a bank account with a partner bank.
- MOA and AOA are filed as e-MOA (INC-33) and e-AOA (INC-34).

## Deadlines after incorporation
| When | What | Basis | If missed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Promptly (no fixed statutory day, but needed for INC-20A) | Open the current account and receive each subscriber's share money | Companies Act s.10A requires proof of subscription received | Cannot file INC-20A |
| Within 30 days | Board appoints the first statutory auditor | s.139(6) | members appoint at an EGM within 90 days; default penalties |
| Within 60 days | Issue share certificates to subscribers | s.56(4) | penalty |
| **Within 180 days** | **INC-20A**: declaration that every subscriber has paid for the shares, with bank proof | s.10A(1) (companies incorporated on or after 2 Nov 2018 with share capital) | company penalty Rs 50,000; each officer in default Rs 1,000 a day up to Rs 1 lakh (s.10A(2), statute wording recalled, verify); company cannot start business or borrow; ROC may strike the company off (s.10A(3)). ROC Pune penalised a late filer in Aug 2026; no extension circular operative in FY 2026-27 |
| Annually | DIR-3 KYC for directors, AOC-4 and MGT-7A/MGT-7, board meetings, ITR, GST returns if registered | various | late fees accrue per day |

## Registrations that depend on your business (from the reel's 12-item list, sorted)
| Item | Needed when | Cost on the government portal |
|---|---|---|
| PAN, TAN | always; come with SPICe+ | included |
| GST registration | aggregate turnover above Rs 20 lakh for services (Rs 10 lakh in special-category states) or Rs 40 lakh for goods in most states; always for inter-state supply of goods and e-commerce sellers; voluntarily if B2B clients want input credit | free |
| Udyam registration ("MSME" is the same thing, not a separate item) | useful for MSME benefits: 45-day payment protection, collateral-free loan schemes, some tenders | free, self-declaration on udyamregistration.gov.in |
| Shop and Establishment | state law; usually within 30-60 days of opening an office (some states exempt home offices or very small units) | small state fee |
| Trade licence | municipal; mainly for premises selling goods or food; many software offices are not covered | municipal fee |
| Bank account | always | free |
| DSC | always for directors (and for filings) | about Rs 1,000-2,500 from a certifying authority (market price) |
| IEC (Import Export Code) | importing or exporting goods; generally not needed for exporting services unless claiming export benefits | free on DGFT |
| PF registration | number comes with SPICe+; compliance from 20 employees | free |
| ESIC registration | number comes with SPICe+; compliance from 10 employees (wage ceiling applies) | free |
| Professional tax | states such as Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal | state fee |
| DPIIT recognition (Startup India) | optional; needed for startup tax holiday applications (separate IMB approval), self-certification under labour and environment laws, some public-procurement relaxations | **free** on startupindia.gov.in |
| D-U-N-S number (Dun & Bradstreet) | publishing apps as an organisation: **required** for an Apple Developer Program company account and a Google Play organisation developer account; optional (may speed review) for Google Ads advertiser verification and monthly invoicing | **free** (see below); D&B sells an expedited service you do not need |

## D-U-N-S number: get it free (added 2026-10-30 from a Hindi reel, checked against Apple and Google help pages)
- It is a 9-digit business identifier from Dun & Bradstreet. A legal entity (company, LLP) needs one before enrolling in the Apple Developer Program or creating a Google Play **organisation** account; an individual or sole proprietor enrols as an individual and does not need one.
- Free route: Apple's D-U-N-S lookup tool (linked from developer.apple.com/support/D-U-N-S; needs a free Apple Account). Search your exact legal entity name; if D&B already has a number, Apple shows or emails it; if not, submit the form and D&B issues one free. Apple says to allow **up to 5 business days** (expediting does not shorten it), plus up to 2 business days before Apple sees it; email D&B if it takes over two weeks. Google warns its route can take up to 30 days.
- The legal name, address and phone must match the incorporation records exactly; trade names, DBAs and branches are not accepted. D&B may phone and ask for registration documents.
- For Brimpa clients: if a clinic or hospital wants its own app listed under its own name, start the D-U-N-S request on day one of the project; it is on the critical path for store submission.

## Name, structure and trademark, in the right order (added 2026-10-30 from an Aug 2026 Hindi reel, fees checked against 2026 trademark-fee guides)
The reel's sheet: (1) pick a name with Namelix or GoDaddy's name tools, check it on ipindia.gov.in; (2) "GST registration" as proprietorship, partnership, LLP or Pvt Ltd; (3) trademark through an advocate, government fee Rs 4,500, "brand legally secure"; (4) register the company on the MCA portal. Corrected order:
1. **Name**: generate candidates (Namelix is free), then check three places the same day: IP India trademark public search (same or similar mark in your class, e.g. class 42 for software and IT services, 35 for business services, 44 for medical services), MCA company/LLP name availability, and the `.com`/`.in` domain plus social handles.
2. **Pick the structure**, not "GST type": sole proprietorship (no MCA filing at all: PAN, bank current account, Udyam, Shop and Establishment, GST only if needed), partnership (deed, optional state registration), LLP (MCA FiLLiP; partners' personal assets are protected), private limited (MCA SPICe+; best for raising money and hiring with ESOPs). See skills/money-guide for the tax comparison.
3. **Register the entity** (MCA for LLP or Pvt Ltd). PAN and TAN come with it.
4. **GST**: separate step, needed only above the threshold or for specific supplies (table above).
5. **Trademark** (form TM-A, per class): government fee **Rs 4,500** e-filed for individuals, sole proprietors, DPIIT-recognised startups and Udyam-registered small enterprises; **Rs 9,000** for other companies and LLPs (Rs 5,000 / Rs 10,000 physical). A Pvt Ltd needs Udyam or DPIIT proof to get Rs 4,500. Advocate or agent fees are extra (market rates vary widely). You can use the TM symbol once you apply; (R) only after registration, which usually takes many months to over a year if unopposed. File before spending on branding.

## The upsell lesson from the founder's story
An agent hired only for incorporation tried to sell DPIIT recognition, Startup India enrolment and similar add-ons at Rs 10,000-30,000 each. These filings are free on the government portals and take an afternoon; they are worth doing only when the benefit applies (for example, a DPIIT certificate before applying to a startup-only tender or grant). Ask the agent for the statutory basis and the government fee of each item before agreeing. Do refuse politely in writing, so there is a record that you were told and chose not to (the founder's worry was "they will say we told you").

## Caveats
- Penalty amounts and thresholds change by notification; check the MCA and GST portals before advising a client.
- GST thresholds are for intra-state supplies by normal taxpayers; composition and special cases differ.
- Not legal advice; involve a CS or CA for the actual filings.

## What the source got wrong
- **"INC-20A must be filed within 120 days"**: the statute says **180 days** from incorporation (s.10A(1)). Wrong number; right warning that the consequences are serious (penalty plus possible strike-off).
- "Within 30 days of the GST number, the bank account must be opened and share capital deposited": no such 30-day rule exists; the practical constraint is that INC-20A (180 days) needs the bank proof, and the first-auditor deadline is the real 30-day one.
- "You need a GST number" as a default incorporation document: GST is required only above the turnover threshold or for specific supplies; many new service companies register voluntarily, but it is not automatic.
- D-U-N-S reel (Sep 2026): "Google and Facebook have made D-U-N-S mandatory for every business" is false as stated: Google Ads says it is **not required** for advertiser verification (it may help); it is mandatory only for Google Play organisation accounts and Apple company enrolment. The Meta claim was not confirmed. "Takes 30 days, express costs Rs 20,000+" describes D&B's paid route; the Apple lookup is free and official (not a "secret link"), and "1 minute" is the form time, not the issue time (up to 5 business days). "This method can close any time" is a follow-and-comment hook.
- "How to make a company" reel (Aug 2026): calls the structure choice "GST registration" (GST is a tax registration, not the business form, and is not needed below the threshold); puts trademark before company registration without saying the Rs 4,500 fee applies to a company only with Udyam or DPIIT proof (otherwise Rs 9,000); says the trademark makes the brand "legally secure" immediately (protection comes on registration; the application gives a priority date); and leaves out the MCA name check. "Namelix and GoDaddy are completely free" is true for name ideas only; domains cost money.
- The on-screen list counts **Udyam and MSME as two items**; they are one registration. It also mixes always-needed items (PAN, TAN, bank, DSC) with situational ones (IEC, trade licence, ESIC) without saying which is which.
