# Compliance ladder by turnover (India, FY 2026-27)

## The point in one line
Most compliance in India is triggered by specific thresholds (GST registration, tax audit, GSTR-9, e-invoice, employee counts), not by vague turnover "bands"; know the exact trigger so you neither over-comply early nor miss one, and budget for a part-time accountant well before Rs 5 crore.

## The ladder (corrected)
| Trigger | What switches on | Basis / note |
|---|---|---|
| Day one of a business | Current account in the business name; banks ask for any one or two business proofs (GST certificate, Shop and Establishment registration, Udyam, partnership deed or incorporation papers) | Udyam is free and useful (MSME 45-day payment protection) but not legally required to open an account |
| Office or clinic premises | State Shops and Establishments registration (many states exempt very small or home units) | state law; small fee |
| Aggregate turnover over **Rs 20 lakh** (services; Rs 10 lakh in special-category states) or **Rs 40 lakh** (goods, most states) | GST registration and monthly/quarterly returns. Up to Rs 5 crore, QRMP lets you file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B quarterly | CGST s.22; see skills/money-guide references/gst-for-it-services.md. Healthcare services by a clinic are largely GST-exempt; the software vendor (Brimpa) is not |
| Tax for the year (after TDS) **Rs 10,000 or more**, at any turnover | Advance tax: 15/45/75/100% by 15 Jun/Sep/Dec/Mar; presumptive filers (s.58, old 44AD/44ADA) pay **all of it by 15 March** in one go | ITA 2025 s.404, s.408 |
| Profession (doctors, IT and other specified professions): gross receipts over **Rs 50 lakh** | Tax audit, unless you stay presumptive under s.58 Sl. 3 with receipts up to Rs 75 lakh and cash at most 5% | s.63; s.62(4) lists "information technology" as a specified profession |
| Business: turnover over **Rs 1 crore** (Rs 10 crore if cash receipts and payments are each at most 5%) | Tax audit (fee for not getting audited: Rs 75,000, Rs 1.5 lakh after one month) | s.63, s.428(c) |
| Individual/HUF whose turnover crossed Rs 1 crore (business) or Rs 50 lakh (profession) in the previous year | Must deduct TDS on contractor, professional-fee, rent and interest payments, deposit it monthly and file quarterly TDS returns | rule under the 1961 Act (194C/194J etc.), carried into s.393 of the 2025 Act [verify the exact clause] |
| Aggregate turnover over **Rs 2 crore** | GSTR-9 annual return becomes compulsory (optional at or below Rs 2 crore) | GST notifications; per money-guide reference |
| Aggregate turnover over **Rs 5 crore** (in any year since 2017-18) | E-invoicing (IRN) for B2B invoices, GSTR-9C reconciliation statement, 6-digit HSN/SAC codes, QRMP no longer available (monthly returns) | GST notifications (e-invoice from 1 Aug 2023 at Rs 5 crore) [verify current notification] |
| **10 or more employees** | ESIC registration and monthly contributions (wage ceiling applies) | Code on Social Security 2020, in force 21 Nov 2025; ESI Act rules continue for a transition year |
| **20 or more employees** | EPF registration and monthly contributions | same Code. A company gets EPFO/ESIC numbers at incorporation via SPICe+, but contributions start only at these counts |

## How to use it for Brimpa and clients
- A solo IT freelancer under Rs 75 lakh receipts, presumptive, with no staff: GST (if over Rs 20 lakh or B2B clients want it), quarterly QRMP returns, one advance-tax payment by 15 March, ITR. No audit, no TDS-deduction duty in the first year.
- A clinic adding staff: the 10-employee ESIC trigger usually comes before any turnover trigger. Put it in the HMS payroll module checklist (domains/hospital-management-modules.md).
- When a client's turnover approaches Rs 5 crore, their billing software must support e-invoice IRN generation and QR codes; that is a feature request Brimpa can sell into.
- Above about Rs 10 crore the reel's advice (hire a strong consultant whose job is growth systems, not just filings) is sensible but generic.

## Caveats
- Thresholds change through Finance Acts and GST notifications; re-check before telling a client a date or number.
- GST thresholds are for intra-state supplies by normal taxpayers; inter-state goods supply and e-commerce sellers must register from the first rupee.
- Not tax or legal advice; a CA should confirm before filings.

## What the source got wrong
- Presents compliance as turnover bands ("40 lakh to 1.5 crore: advance tax four times a year"). Advance tax depends on the **tax amount** (Rs 10,000 or more), not on turnover, and presumptive filers pay once, by 15 March.
- "Income tax over 1 crore means tax audit": the trigger is **turnover** over Rs 1 crore for a business (Rs 10 crore with at most 5% cash), and **gross receipts over Rs 50 lakh for professionals**, which is the one that matters for IT freelancers and doctors. Presumptive filers within limits are exempt.
- "From 1.5 crore, GSTR-9 is mandatory": the threshold is **Rs 2 crore**.
- "Need both Udyam and Shop Act for a current account": banks ask for one or two business proofs; Udyam is optional.
- The 5-10 crore band (e-invoice, GSTR-9C) and the 10/20 employee thresholds for ESIC/EPF are correct.
- "Full-time accountant and billing/inventory software at 1.5-5 crore" is reasonable advice, not a legal requirement.
