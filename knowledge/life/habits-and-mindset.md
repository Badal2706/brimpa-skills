# Habits and mindset

## The point in one line
The five challenges are sensible 2-4 week experiments; the boiling-frog story is biologically false but its career lesson (review where you are every year) holds.

## Five solo challenges (as on the cards)
| Challenge | Card says | Check |
|---|---|---|
| No sugar | 30 days, no chocolate, soda or hidden sugar in sauces and juices; first 10 days hard, by day 20 skin, energy and clarity change | Cutting added sugar is well supported for weight and metabolic health; the day-by-day effects are anecdotal. Fruit is fine |
| 3 L water rule | 3 L every day for 21 days; most people are dehydrated "and call it anxiety"; the brain is about 75% water | Brain water content is about right. Need varies: reference intakes are about 2.5 L (EFSA) to 3.7 L (US) of **total** water a day for men, including food. 3 L of drinks suits an Indian summer or active day; urine colour is the simple check. Mild dehydration can affect mood, but "it's not anxiety, it's thirst" is overstated |
| No screens after 10 pm | Phone down at 10 for 14 days, no reels, texts or videos; sleep deepens | Reasonable. The evidence is stronger for stimulating content and later bedtimes than for blue light alone |
| Daily meditation | 10 minutes, no app, eyes closed, breathe; the first 3 days the mind "goes crazy"; do 21 days | Meta-analyses show small-to-moderate benefits for stress and anxiety; the restlessness at the start is normal |
| The 3-hour disappear | Once a week, 3 hours with no phone, wifi or people; walk, think | Harmless and useful for thinking time; for a founder, a weekly no-input block is where plans get made |

## The boiling frog (career)
Claim: a frog dropped into boiling water jumps out, but one in slowly heated water adjusts until it dies. Likewise a career declines slowly (late promotions, skipped courses, ignored feedback, applications not sent) until, three years later, you are doing the same work in the same place.
Check: the biology is a **myth**; frogs in gradually heated water try to escape once it gets uncomfortable. The lesson is still useful. A concrete version: once a year write down what you can do now that you could not a year ago, what you earn versus the market rate, and one move (skill, client type, price) for the next year.

## The observer idea (J. Krishnamurti, Hindi clip)
You can watch your thoughts and your body, so you are not only them; the clip extends this to "you are the consciousness the universe arises in" (an Advaita-style reading). Useful in practice as a stress tool: noticing a thought as a thought (as in meditation) loosens its hold. The metaphysics is philosophy, not something to verify.
