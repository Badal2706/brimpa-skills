# Buying notes

## The point in one line
For a ring that lasts: band at least about 2 mm thick, a cathedral setting if durability matters, and diamonds only part-way round so the ring can be resized.

## Engagement ring: three rules from a jewellery designer
1. **Band at least 2 mm thick.** Gold is soft, and thin bands (under about 1.8-2 mm) bend and go out of round. Widely repeated jeweller guidance; 22k gold, common in India, is softer than 14k/18k, so thickness matters more.
2. **Cathedral setting over a simple mount** if you want it structurally sound: the arches that hold the head give the stone more support points (the designer says three versus one). Trade-off: sits higher and can catch on clothes.
3. **Diamond band only about 75% of the way round** (a three-quarter eternity band). The plain part at the bottom can be cut to resize; a full eternity band usually has to be remade.
Also ask for the hallmark (BIS HUID on gold in India) and a lab report for the main diamond (IGI or GIA; say whether it is lab-grown).

## Men's everyday essentials (list as shown)
Full-length mirror; perfume cabinet; EDC (everyday-carry) tray for keys, wallet and watch; wooden hangers (keep jacket and shirt shape); dopp kit (toiletry bag, heard as "dock gate"); shoe rack; clothes brush (lint and dust); shoe horn (protects the heel counter). A shopping list, not advice; buy only what solves a problem you have.
