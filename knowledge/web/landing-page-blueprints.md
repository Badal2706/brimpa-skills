# Two default page blueprints

## The point in one line
A product page and a business/agency site need different section sets; use these as checklists of what a visitor may look for, then cut and reorder by the argument (web-design skill), not as a fixed template.

## Product page (11)
1 Header, 2 Hero, 3 Problem, 4 Features, 5 Benefits, 6 How it works, 7 Social proof, 8 Pricing, 9 FAQ, 10 Final CTA, 11 Footer.

## Business / agency site (15)
1 Navbar, 2 Hero, 3 Trusted by, 4 About us, 5 Services, 6 Why us, 7 Stats, 8 Process, 9 Portfolio, 10 Team, 11 Testimonials, 12 Pricing, 13 FAQ, 14 Contact, 15 Footer.

## How this fits our skills
- The web-design skill orders sections by the argument (claim then proof) and deletes sections without a claim, so not every page uses every block; use these lists to check nothing a buyer needs is missing.
- "Trusted by" and "Stats" need real logos and numbers (never invented); "Team" needs real photos.
- Local businesses (clinics, showrooms) add hours, map and a direct call/WhatsApp action near the top.
