# Freelancer money system (India)

## The point in one line
Run three accounts (business, tax vault, personal). Pay yourself a fixed salary. On every receipt, move all of the GST and an income-tax percentage into the vault. Build 9-12 months of essentials before investing aggressively.

## The system
1. **Accounts.**
   - Business current account: all client money in, all business costs out.
   - Tax vault (separate savings account or sweep FD): the GST plus the income-tax bucket.
   - Personal account: one fixed monthly salary transfer.
2. **Salary to yourself.** 70-80% of the trailing 12-month average net income (receipts minus costs minus tax). Cut it quickly after weak months; raise it slowly. A quarterly bonus only when the buffer is full.
3. **Tax buckets on every receipt.**
   - **GST:** 100% of the GST collected.
   - **Income tax:** the effective rate on receipts plus about 1 point, minus TDS already deducted.

     | Receipts (presumptive, new regime) | Effective rate | Bucket |
     |---|---|---|
     | Up to Rs 24 lakh | 0% | 0-1% |
     | Rs 30 lakh | 3.6% | 5% |
     | Rs 40 lakh | 5.2% | 6% |
     | Rs 50 lakh | 6.9% | 8% |
     | Rs 75 lakh | 9.8% | 11% |

   - **On books with high profit:** about 30% of profit.
4. **Emergency fund.** 9-12 months of essentials (a salaried person needs about 6), plus 2-3 months of business costs. Keep it in a sweep FD or liquid fund. Get health insurance first.
5. **Budget the salary, not the receipts.** 50% needs / 30% wants / 20% saving; 50/20/30 while the emergency fund is being built.
6. **Debt order.**
   - Credit card in full every month (about 36-45% a year if revolved).
   - Then personal loans and BNPL.
   - Prepay a home loan only after the fund and insurance are in place. Self-occupied home-loan interest gives no benefit in the new regime.
7. **Invoicing.**
   - SOW with milestones and a 30-50% advance from new clients.
   - Invoice the day a milestone is accepted; Net 7-15.
   - GST on a separate line; bank or UPI only.
   - Reconcile client TDS with AIS every quarter.
8. **Investing.**
   - An index-fund SIP the day after the salary transfer.
   - PPF (7.1% for Oct-Dec 2026, tax-free) for safe money.
   - Harvest Rs 1.25 lakh of LTCG every year.
9. **Insurance.**
   - Family floater of Rs 10-25 lakh plus a super top-up; a separate policy for parents.
   - Term cover of 10-15x expenses plus loans if anyone depends on you.
   - Professional indemnity and cyber cover for software contracts that touch patient data.
10. **Scams to refuse.**
    - "Digital arrest" calls.
    - Trading tip groups and fake IPO apps.
    - Deposit-first "task" jobs.
    - UPI collect requests (a PIN is never needed to receive money).
    - Fake tax or GST notices (check the portal).
    - Overpaying clients who want a refund.
    - Report on 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in.

## Example: one Rs 1,00,000 invoice (Rs 45 lakh a year, so a bucket of about 7%)
- **Company client deducting 10% TDS:** pays Rs 1,08,000 (Rs 1,18,000 minus Rs 10,000 TDS). Rs 18,000 goes to the GST vault and nothing to the income-tax bucket, because TDS already covers it.
- **Clinic, no TDS:** pays Rs 1,18,000. Rs 18,000 goes to the GST vault and Rs 7,000 to the income-tax bucket.
- **Foreign client under LUT:** pays Rs 1,00,000 with no GST. Rs 7,000 goes to the income-tax bucket.

## Legal limits the system protects
- No cash receipt of Rs 2 lakh or more from one person, transaction or event (s.186). The penalty is 100% of the amount (s.451).
- Cash above 5% of receipts loses the Rs 75 lakh presumptive limit (s.58).
- A presumptive filer pays all advance tax by 15 March (s.408(2)).
- The RBI repo rate was 5.50% as of 30 Oct 2026, after a 25 bp increase at the October MPC, so repo-linked EMIs rise.

## Caveats
- The months, percentages and splits are rules of thumb, not law. Adjust them to real expenses and the volatility of income.
- Bucket percentages assume s.58 Sl. 3 presumptive, the new regime and no other income. Salary plus freelance needs the marginal rate instead.
- Not investment advice. No specific funds or insurers are recommended.
