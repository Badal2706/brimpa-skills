# Credit card reward claims: how to check them

## The point in one line
A reel's "16-24% back" is almost always best-case maths (portal-only multipliers, points valued at airline-transfer rates, caps ignored, fees and forex markup left out); compute net value yourself from the bank's own terms page, and only after the card is paid in full every month.

## The formula
Net value for a year = (eligible spend x points per Rs 100 / 100 x realistic rupee value per point) + welcome/milestone benefits - annual fee incl. 18% GST - forex markup incl. GST on foreign spends - convenience fees (tax, rent, utilities) - interest if ever revolved.
- **Realistic point value**: what you would actually redeem for (statement credit or a flight you were going to buy anyway), not the best transfer partner on a premium route.
- **Caps**: accelerated rates usually stop at a monthly or per-cycle cap; spend above the cap earns the base rate.
- **Portal-only rates**: multipliers that apply only when booking through the bank's travel portal may come with higher fares than booking direct.
- Revolving a balance costs about 36-45% a year (money-guide), which wipes out any reward rate. Rewards are only worth chasing for a card cleared in full every month.

## HSBC TravelOne vs Scapia (the reel's Rs 1.5 lakh trip: Rs 50k flights, Rs 50k hotels, Rs 50k spent abroad)
Verified on the official pages (2026-10-30):
- **HSBC TravelOne**: joining and annual fee Rs 4,999 (+18% GST = Rs 5,899; the reel's "5,900" is this); annual fee waived above Rs 8 lakh spend a year. Earns **4 points per Rs 100 on flights, travel aggregators and foreign-currency spends**, 2 per Rs 100 elsewhere; 10,000 bonus points above Rs 12 lakh a year. Points convert 1:1 to airline miles in the app. Welcome: Rs 1,000 cashback + Rs 3,000 voucher on Rs 25,000 spend in 30 days (offer to 31 Mar 2027).
- **Scapia** (lifetime free): **0% forex markup** (official site, from 27 Feb 2026); about 10 coins per Rs 100 general spend and 20 per Rs 100 on in-app travel bookings, 5 coins = Rs 1, so about 2% and 4%; coins redeem only on bookings inside the Scapia app; utilities and insurance stopped earning from 27 Feb 2026 (third-party).

Recomputed on the bank's own rates (direct bookings, points at Rs 1):
| | Scapia | HSBC TravelOne |
|---|---|---|
| Flights Rs 50k | about Rs 2,000 (in-app booking) | 2,000 points |
| Hotels Rs 50k | about Rs 2,000 (in-app booking) | 1,000-2,000 points (2 or 4 per Rs 100 depending on how the merchant codes) |
| Rs 50k abroad | 0% markup; coin earning on foreign spend not confirmed | 2,000 points, but forex markup reported at 3.5% (+GST) = about Rs 2,065 |
| Fee | 0 | Rs 5,899 (first year offset by Rs 4,000 welcome benefit) |
| Net, year 1 | about Rs 4,000-6,000 | about Rs 1,000-2,000 incl. welcome benefit; negative in later years at this spend |

The reel's 22,000 points (8,000 on flights, 12,000 on hotels) needs the bank's **rewards-marketplace multipliers** (third-party sites quote 16 points per Rs 100 on flights and 24 on hotels, with monthly caps quoted as 18,000 or 50,000 points - sources conflict). If those portal rates and fares hold for your trip, TravelOne wins for a heavy traveller; if you book direct, Scapia wins at this spend. Transfer value (for example "enough for a round trip to Kuala Lumpur") depends on award availability and changes without notice.

## For Brimpa / Badal
- Paying advance tax or GST by card is legal and can earn points; the convenience fee is a business cost. Worth it only if net points value exceeds the fee, the card is cleared in full, and the cap is not already used. Never pay tax late to chase a card cycle (interest under the tax law costs more).
- Keep business and personal cards separate (same rule as separate bank accounts in finance/freelancer-money-system.md).
- Each application is a hard credit enquiry; do not open cards in bunches before a home or business loan.

## Caveats
- Card terms in India change several times a year (devaluations, new exclusions, fee changes). Every number here is as of 2026-10-30; re-check the bank's Most Important Terms and Conditions (MITC) before applying.
- Both reels carry affiliate links ("apply through the link in my bio"); the creator earns per approval.
- Whether reward points on business spends are taxable is not settled here; ask the CA if the amounts become material.

## What the source got wrong
- "Scapia gives you nothing on Rs 50k spent abroad": its main benefit there is 0% forex markup (it saves about Rs 2,000 vs a 3.5% + GST card); coin earning on foreign spends was not confirmed either way.
- HSBC forex "extra charge Rs 315" on Rs 50,000: third-party sources put the markup at 3.5% (+GST), about Rs 2,065; the official page does not state it. Treat Rs 2,065 as the planning figure until the MITC says otherwise.
- "Even at Rs 1 a point your net gain is Rs 15,785, 2.5x Scapia": only with portal-only multipliers on every rupee and no caps; on direct bookings the net is a few thousand rupees.
- "12-18% back on fuel" (Times Black): fuel is reported as an excluded category; the benefit is a surcharge waiver.
- Percentages like "16-24%" mix earn rate with a premium transfer valuation; they are not cashback.

## Conflicts and recommendation
- TravelOne hotel/flight portal multipliers and caps differ across review sites (hotels 24 vs 48 per Rs 100; cap 18,000 vs 50,000 points a month). **Recommendation:** use the base rates from the bank's own page for planning and treat portal bonuses as upside.
- Forex markup: reel implies about 0.6%; third parties say 3.5%. **Recommendation:** plan on 3.5% + GST.
