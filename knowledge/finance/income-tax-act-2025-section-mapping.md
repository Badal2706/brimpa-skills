# Income-tax Act 2025: old-to-new section mapping

## The point in one line
From 1 Apr 2026 the Income-tax Act, 2025 replaces the 1961 Act. Income from that date uses the new section numbers, while FY 2025-26 returns and assessments still use the old ones (savings clause, s.536(2)). Always quote both, for example "s.58 Table Sl. 3 (old 44ADA)".

## Key facts
- **In force:** 1 April 2026 (s.1(3)). The 1961 Act is repealed by s.536(1). The 1961 Act still governs every tax year that began before 1 Apr 2026.
- **"Tax year" (s.3)** replaces "previous year" and "assessment year". TY 2026-27 = FY 1 Apr 2026 to 31 Mar 2027.
- **Rules:** the Income-tax Rules, 2026 were notified on 20 Mar 2026 (G.S.R. 198(E)). Per secondary sources: Form 16 becomes 130, 26AS becomes 168, and the audit forms become Form 26.
- **No rate change:** Budget 2026 (1 Feb 2026) made no change to income-tax slabs for TY 2026-27.
- **Beware bad mappings:** some third-party tables map 44ADA to s.59. That is wrong, because s.59 covers non-resident royalties and FTS. Use the Act text.

## Example
A freelancer asks about "44ADA for FY 2026-27". The answer cites s.58 Table Sl. 3 of the 2025 Act (50% deemed profit, Rs 50 / 75 lakh limits). For their FY 2025-26 return, filed in 2026, the answer still cites 44ADA of the 1961 Act.
