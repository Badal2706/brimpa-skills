# Founder content idea bank

## The point in one line
Never start from a blank page: pick one prompt from this bank, attach one real story or number, and post. Use with the launch-marketing skill for hooks and de-slopping.

## The prompts, grouped
**Origin and belief:** why you started; why you still do this; what success means to you; your fear, then and now; you, five years from now; a rule you never break; a promise you keep.
**Clients and proof:** your first client story; a client question, answered; what a client said; a thank-you to a client; how you pick clients; what you say no to; a before and after; a number you are proud of.
**Work and process:** your process in three steps; behind the scenes; a day in your week; your team at work; your morning routine; one tool you pay for; your first office; your weekend.
**Lessons:** a mistake that cost you; a lesson from a bad month; what you'd do differently; what you learnt this week; a book that helped you; a tip for beginners.
**Opinion and engagement:** a myth in your industry; a trend you disagree with; a question you get daily; your price, explained; a reply to a comment.

## How to use it (Brimpa)
- Rotate groups so a week has one proof post, one process post and one opinion post.
- Every post carries one specific detail (a real number, a named tool, a client sector), not a generality.
- Client stories need the client's consent; health clients (clinics, hospitals) never with patient details.
