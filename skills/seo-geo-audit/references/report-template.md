# Report template, scoring and priorities

Scores are audit heuristics (weights adapted from the claude-seo project), not Google signals. Print what was measured and what was not; a category you did not measure is "not measured", never a number.

## Health score weights (0-100)

| Category | Weight | What feeds it |
|---|---|---|
| Technical SEO | 22 | crawlability, indexability, canonicals, robots, sitemap, redirects, JS rendering, HTTPS |
| Content quality | 23 | Who/How/Why, information gain, E-E-A-T signals, freshness, YMYL compliance, thin/duplicate pages |
| On-page | 20 | titles, descriptions, H1/headings, internal links, keyword map / cannibalisation, local signals on page |
| Structured data | 10 | correct specific types, required properties, validation, parity with page and GBP, no retired types relied on |
| Core Web Vitals | 10 | field LCP/INP/CLS on mobile for key templates |
| AI search readiness | 10 | indexable + snippet-eligible, crawler access by purpose, SSR, citability, authorship, measurement in place |
| Images | 5 | alt coverage, dimensions, formats, weight, lazy-loading, LCP image handling |

Per-category score = share of applicable checks passed, pulled down by severity (any Critical in a category caps it at 40; any High caps it at 70). The caps apply only in the root cause's home category: a Critical that also shows symptoms elsewhere (a catch-all route that breaks Technical and also floods Structured data with junk) caps Technical alone, because report rule 2 counts one cause once. Weighted sum over measured categories only, re-normalised to 100, with the list of unmeasured categories printed next to the score.

Checks per category (count passed over applicable, so "share passed" is the same between auditors; add checks only when the site type needs them and say so):
- Technical: robots.txt 200 and not blocking; sitemap 200 with canonical 200 URLs only; one host and scheme, others 301/308 in one hop; self-canonical on every template; no stray `noindex`; real 404s; key content in the initial HTML; certificate valid for every served host.
- Content: Who/How/Why visible; information gain on key pages; coverage floors met; dates honest; YMYL claims reviewed; no thin or duplicate pages.
- On-page: unique title and description per page; one H1 matching the title; heading order; no cannibalised primary phrase; 3+ internal links into key pages; no orphans.
- Structured data: most specific type; required properties present; parses with no errors; every value visible on the page and equal to GBP; no self-serving ratings.
- Core Web Vitals: field LCP, INP and CLS "good" at p75 on mobile for each key template.
- AI readiness: indexable and snippet-eligible; search crawlers allowed by purpose; Search generative AI on Include; named authors; citable answer blocks; a measurement baseline.
- Images: alt on meaningful images; width and height set; modern formats; weight within budget; LCP image eager with `fetchpriority`.

**Show the arithmetic** under the scorecard, every measured term written out (zeros included) and the divisor equal to the sum of the measured weights only. Example for the scorecard rendering at the end of this file, CWV not measured: `(22x78 + 23x61 + 20x72 + 10x40 + 10x80 + 5x55) / 90 = 6034 / 90 = 67.0 -> 67`. Re-add the products before publishing; a total that does not follow from the printed terms discredits the whole report.

## Report rules (keep these; they are what makes the report trustworthy)

1. **Score only measured categories.** A category you could not measure is printed as "not measured" with the reason and left out of both the numerator and the divisor. Never estimate it, never fill it with a neutral 50, never fold a guess into the total.
2. **Severity follows the root cause.** When several symptoms come from one cause (a catch-all route returning `200` for every path shows up as soft 404s, junk sitemap URLs and HTML served at `/llms.txt`), write one finding for the cause at the cause's severity and list the symptoms under it. Do not rate each symptom Critical, and do not count one cause several times against the score.
3. **Every finding carries a `Failed if:` line**, Critical to Info, KEEP included: one command or observable condition with a clear pass/fail (bank in `references/technical-checklist.md` section 17). For KEEP and Info it is the regression condition.
4. **Client-facing language only.** No internal tool, skill, agent or model names ("security-review skill" becomes "a security review"), no "generated by" or promotional footers, no links to the auditor's own products. Name third-party tools only when the client needs them to run a check (Search Console, PageSpeed Insights, Rich Results Test).
5. **Evidence before verdict.** Each finding cites the raw value seen; anything not seen is "not measured" or "inferred", labelled as such.

Local businesses get a second score, **Local visibility (0-100)**: GBP signals 25, reviews and reputation 20, local on-page 20, NAP and citations 15, local schema 10, local links and authority 10. It is re-normalised over the parts actually measured, exactly like the health score (no GBP access means GBP signals are "not measured" and leave the divisor), with the arithmetic and the unmeasured parts printed. GBP completeness itself is scored with the 25-field checklist in `references/local-seo.md`.

Optional **Conversion and copy lens** (from the position-me teardown method), five dimensions 0-100 reported separately and never mixed into the health score: visual hierarchy (LIFT: value, relevance, clarity, anxiety, distraction, urgency; Hick's law for choices per screen), copywriting (problem-agitate-solve in the hero, verbs in CTAs, microcopy), content actionability (fluff test), technical SEO and schema, AI/answer-engine parseability.

## Priority levels and SLAs

| Priority | Meaning | Fix by |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | blocks indexing, violates a spam policy, breaks the site for users, or exposes the owner (fake reviews, hidden text, YMYL misinformation) | immediately |
| High | significantly affects rankings or conversions on key pages | within 1 week |
| Medium | optimisation opportunity | within 1 month |
| Low | nice to have | backlog |
| Info | aware, no action (retired schema present, `priority` in sitemap) | none |

## Finding record (one per issue)

```
### <ID> <short title>                     Priority: <Critical|High|Medium|Low|Info>
Verdict: KEEP | MODIFY | REMOVE | DO NOT TOUCH
Where: <URL(s) or template>
Root cause: <the one cause; symptoms of the same cause are listed here, not filed as separate findings>
Observation: <what was actually seen; include the raw value, e.g. "<title>Home</title>", "robots.txt returns 503">
Why it matters: <one sentence, cite the Google doc or the user impact>
Fix: <exact change; code or GBP field>
Depends on / unblocks: <other IDs>
Failed if: <one command or observable condition, mandatory on every finding, e.g.
  "curl -s -o /dev/null -w '%{http_code}' https://www.example.com/no-such-page/ prints anything but 404",
  "URL Inspection still shows Excluded by noindex 14 days after the fix">
Leading indicator: <what to watch, e.g. impressions for the page in GSC>
Evidence level: <measured | inferred | third-party study (name, date) | not measured>
```

A finding without a `Failed if:` line is not finished. "Monitor rankings" or "check it looks right" cannot fail and does not count.

KEEP = correct, leave it (say why, so nobody "optimises" it later). MODIFY = keep the element, change the value. REMOVE = delete (unused files, duplicate schema blocks, meta keywords, doorway pages). DO NOT TOUCH = working but fragile or outside scope (payment flows, legal pages, tracking that compliance depends on, redirects from an old domain).

## Action matrix (compact alternative for small sites)

| Severity | Category | Problem | Fix | Effort | Evidence | Failed if |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | Technical | robots.txt `Disallow: /` left from staging | remove line, resubmit sitemap, inspect 10 URLs | 10 min | measured | `curl -s https://www.example.com/robots.txt` still shows `Disallow: /` under `User-agent: *` |
| High | Local | GBP primary category "Doctor" instead of "Pulmonologist" | change category, keep "Doctor" as secondary | 5 min | measured | the public Business Profile still shows "Doctor" as the first category after 3 days |
| Medium | On-page | 14 service pages share the description "Contact us today" | write page-specific descriptions with price-from / area | 2 h | measured | the per-page on-page table still shows two or more pages with the same description |

**Compact client report** (for a small site or a client who will not read a long report; under about 2,000 words): the executive summary block from the skeleton (score with arithmetic, not-measured list, top issues, KEEP list), then this action matrix as the whole findings section with every row keeping its `Failed if`, then the action plan by week and the limitations. Full finding records stay in the auditor's working file and are sent on request. Switch to the full skeleton when there are more than about 25 findings or any Critical needs a root-cause explanation longer than one row.

Never fill an "expected ROI" column with invented percentages. If a third-party study is the basis, name it and its date; otherwise describe the expected effect qualitatively.

## Share-of-voice table (AI visibility baseline)

| Prompt | ChatGPT | Perplexity | Gemini / AI Mode | Claude | Our rank | Domains cited |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| "best pulmonologist in Indirapuram" | mentioned (#2) | not mentioned | mentioned (#1) | not mentioned | 1.5 | practo.com, justdial.com, example-clinic.in |

Summary metrics (geo-gap-fixer rubric): mention rate = prompts with a brand mention / prompts; average rank = mean first-mention position among tracked brands; win rate = share of prompts where the brand is first; citation share = brand-domain citations / all citations. Bands (heuristic): mention rate 80-100 excellent, 50-79 good, 20-49 weak, below 20 critical; citation share 20%+ strong, 5-19 moderate, under 5 weak. Point-in-time only; re-run monthly with the same prompts.
