## 3. GST, recurring [verify]

**Monthly filer:**
- GSTR-1 by the 11th.
- GSTR-3B and payment by the 20th.

**QRMP filer (aggregate turnover up to Rs 5 crore):**
- Months 1 and 2: IFF (optional) by the 13th, PMT-06 payment by the 25th.

## 5. Company or LLP [verify]

- **Company:** DIR-3 KYC and the AGM by 30 Sep; AOC-4 within 30 days of the AGM; MGT-7/7A within 60 days of the AGM.
- **LLP:** Form 11 by 30 May; Form 8 by 30 Oct.
- **A new private limited company, counted from the incorporation date:**
  - First auditor appointed by the board within **30 days** (Companies Act 2013 s.139(6); if the board does not, the members appoint at an EGM within 90 days). Whether ADT-1 must be filed for a first auditor is disputed between sources: ask the CS.
  - Share certificates to the subscribers within **two months** (s.56(4)(a)), whether or not the subscription money has been paid. A company that is not a small company must also follow the demat rule (Rule 9B).
  - **INC-20A** declaration of commencement of business within **180 days** (s.10A), after the subscription money is in the company's bank account; the company may not start business or borrow before it is filed.
  - Basis: section numbers and periods agree across secondary legal sources, including a ROC adjudication order under s.56(4)(a), as of Oct 2026. The MCA text of the Act could not be fetched, so confirm with your CS.

## 6. How to present deadlines to the user

- List only the next 3-5 dates that apply to their situation, nearest first, with what to pay or file and roughly how much.
- Mark any date that is [verify] or per secondary sources, and tell them to confirm extensions on the portal.
- **For a presumptive freelancer on 30 Oct 2026, the next dates are:**
  - 31 Dec 2026: belated FY 2025-26 return, if missed.
  - The quarterly GST dates.
  - 15 Mar 2027: the full advance tax for TY 2026-27.
  - 31 Mar 2027: year-end moves.
  - 31 Aug 2027: the TY 2026-27 return.
