# Sources

This skill distils two kinds of material: nineteen practitioner videos on website, landing-page, product-page, pricing and premium-brand psychology (summarised and adapted; no transcript text reproduced beyond short quoted phrases; creator promotions, sponsors and course plugs dropped; every number from a video is labelled "as claimed in the source video"), and academic and industry research on first impressions, luxury strategy, attention and persuasion (cited, with evidence grades in `references/evidence.md`). Statements dated "as of Oct 2026" reflect the state of these sources when the skill was written (2026-10-22).

## 3. Licence and attribution notes

- Videos: summarised and adapted, not reproduced. Only publicly stated cases, rules of thumb and short quoted phrases were used; no conversion statistic from a video is presented as a fact in this skill, and each is labelled "as claimed in the source video". Creator promotions, sponsor segments, course plugs and booking links were left out. Channel names and titles are given for attribution only.
- Papers and reports: cited with their published figures and the caveats recorded in `references/evidence.md`; nothing is reproduced beyond short paraphrase. Where a popular figure could not be traced to a primary study it is listed as a myth rather than cited.
- Industry data (Bain, McKinsey, Baymard, Contentsquare, web.dev, Shopify) is quoted as context with its date; re-verify yearly, as these figures age fastest.
- The web-design skill in this repository owns the direction catalog, house rules, section and motion mechanics and the pre-flight that this skill references; its own sources are in `skills/web-design/references/sources.md`. Repo-level credits live in `NOTICE` and `SOURCES.md`.
