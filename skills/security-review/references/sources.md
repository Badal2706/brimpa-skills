# Sources and attribution

This skill is released under MIT. It merges, adapts and links the following upstream material. Exploit payloads, attacker tradecraft and tool-specific marketing in the sources were deliberately left out; everything here is phrased as audit checks and secure configuration.

| Source | URL | License | What was taken |
|---|---|---|---|
| cloudflare/security-audit-skill | https://github.com/cloudflare/security-audit-skill | MIT (Copyright 2025-2026 Cloudflare, Inc.) | Merged and rephrased: the per-category checklist structure and most items in `checklist.md` (authentication, sessions, authorization, injection, uploads, secrets, errors, availability, crypto, headers, cloud/CI, supply chain, data lifecycle, messaging, client-side); the "boundary and result" finding definition; severity anchors (critical/high/medium/low/informational) and the high/medium discriminator; the confirmed / needs-validation / rejected verdict model; execution-safety rules (read-only source, bounded local evidence, never probe production); anti-patterns; report structure. |
